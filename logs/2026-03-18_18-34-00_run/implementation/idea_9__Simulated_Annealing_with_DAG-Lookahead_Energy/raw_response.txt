- Proposes random logical-qubit pair swap → updates only affected gate distances.
- Accepts by `ΔE < 0` or `exp(-ΔE/T)`.
- Tracks global best mapping; restores it at end.

**Bijectivity guarantee**: Only swap operations on `(mapping, reverse_mapping)` are used — the permutation invariant is never broken.
