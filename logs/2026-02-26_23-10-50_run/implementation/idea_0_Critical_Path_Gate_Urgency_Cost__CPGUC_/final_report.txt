## Description: Weights each gate's routing cost by its position on the circuit's **critical path** — the longest dependency chain from that gate to the circuit output. Precompute CP(g) = length of longest directed path from g to circuit output in the gate DAG. Normalize: w_g = CP(g) / max_{g'} CP(g').
