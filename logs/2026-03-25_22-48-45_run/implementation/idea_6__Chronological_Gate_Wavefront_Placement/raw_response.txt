**Chronological Gate Wavefront Placement** — the `init_mapping(self)` function:

1. **DAG Construction & Topological Sort** — Builds a dependency DAG from `self.access` using per-qubit last-gate tracking, then performs Kahn's algorithm for topological ordering.

2. **Future Interaction Counting** — Pre-computes how many 2-qubit gates each logical qubit participates in, used to prioritize placement.

3. **Wavefront Placement** — Processes gates in topological order:
   - **Both qubits new**: Finds the best available hardware edge (highest combined unoccupied neighbor degree), assigns higher-interaction qubit to higher-degree endpoint.
   - **One qubit placed**: Places the new qubit on the nearest unoccupied neighbor of the already-placed qubit, preferring neighbors with more flexibility.
   - **Single-qubit gate, new qubit**: Places at the physical qubit with most unoccupied neighbors.
   - **Both placed**: Skip.

4. **Remaining Qubits** — Any unplaced logical qubits get assigned to remaining physical qubits.

5. **2-opt Local Search Refinement** — Computes layer-decayed interaction weights, then iteratively swaps physical assignments to minimize QAP cost until convergence (up to 50 passes).

6. **Output** — Sets `self.mapping_dict` (logical→physical) and `self.reverse_mapping_dict` (physical→logical) as lists indexed by qubit ID.
