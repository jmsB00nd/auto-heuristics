---

**Entanglement Concentration Hub Mapping (ECHM)**

**EC Score** per logical qubit:
```
EC[q] = total_weight[q] × log₂(1 + diversity[q]) × (1 + depth_criticality[q])
```
- `total_weight`: how many 2-qubit gates involve `q`
- `diversity`: number of unique interaction partners (log-dampened — diminishing returns)
- `depth_criticality`: Σ `1/(1 + norm_gate_pos)` over all 2q gates of `q` — early gates contribute more

**Hub Score** per physical qubit (closeness centrality):
```
hub[p] = n_reachable / Σ BFS_dist(p, o)
```

**Placement** (EC-descending order):
1. Highest-EC logical → highest-hub physical
2. Each subsequent qubit maximises a **joint score**:
   ```
   joint[p] = proximity(lq, p) + hub[p] × EC[lq] × unplaced_fraction(lq)
   ```
   - `proximity` = Σ `w(lq,lq') / (1 + dist(p, p'))` for placed partners `lq'`
   - `unplaced_fraction` adaptively shifts weight: hub dominates when partners aren't yet placed (need maximum routing flexibility), proximity dominates once partners are seated

**Complexity:** O(|L|² × |P|) — polynomial, no exponential search.
