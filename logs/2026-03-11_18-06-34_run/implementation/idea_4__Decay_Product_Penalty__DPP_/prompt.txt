Idea:  Decay Product Penalty (DPP)
Desc: **
Replaces the baseline's `max(γ_p, γ_q)` decay aggregation with the *product* `γ_p · γ_q`. This changes the fundamental nature of the penalty: while `max` only penalizes the hotter qubit (cold qubit is irrelevant), the product penalizes *both* qubits proportionally to their individual heat. The product also has super-linear growth when both qubits are hot simultaneously — as occurs in SWAP ping-pong — making repeated alternating use of the same qubit pair far more expensive than using either qubit individually. A geometric mean variant `(γ_p · γ_q)^0.5` provides a scaled version.

**MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION:**
```
f_score = Σ_{g ∈ FL} w(g) · d_s(g) / |FL|
e_score = Σ_{g ∈ EL} w(g) · d_s(g) / (|EL| · (ι(g) + 1))  [if |EL| > 0, else 0]

# Replace max with product:
H(s) = (γ_p · γ_q) · (f_score + W · e_score)

# Optional: geometric mean variant for weaker penalty:
H(s) = sqrt(γ_p · γ_q) · (f_score + W · e_score)
```

**LOOP_PREVENTION_STRATEGY:**
Consider a ping-pong loop between SWAPs (p,q) and (q,r). After the first SWAP, γ_p and γ_q both increase by 0.001. After the second SWAP, γ_q and γ_r increase. The shared qubit q accumulates heat from *both* directions. With product aggregation, the cost of SWAP (p,q) is proportional to `γ_p · γ_q`, where γ_q is growing faster than in the baseline (it participates in every other step of the loop). This creates super-linear discouragement of any qubit that acts as a pivot in alternating patterns — precisely the structure of ping-pong oscillation.

**WHY_NOVEL:**
The baseline uses `max` (hot-qubit-dominant), SABRE uses `max`. No existing heuristic in this codebase uses the *product* of individual qubit decays as the aggregation. Product aggregation has a fundamentally different optimization landscape: it creates a "product manifold" of qubit costs rather than a max-manifold, distributing the penalty across both participants and making shared-qubit loops super-linearly expensive. This is a minimal change with non-trivial mathematical consequences.

def qlosure_poly_heuristic(self, swap_gate):
        W = 1
        front_layer_size = len(self.front_layer)
        extended_layer_size = len(self.extended_layer)

        max_decay = max(self.decay_parameter[swap_gate[0]], self.decay_parameter[swap_gate[1]])

        f_distance = 0

        for g in self.front_layer:
            q1, q2 = self.access2q[g]
            Q1, Q2 = self.temp_mapping_dict[q1], self.temp_mapping_dict[q2]
            deps = self.dag_dependencies_count[g]

            f_distance += (deps+1) * self.distance_matrix[Q1][Q2]

        e_distance = 0
        for g in self.extended_layer:
            q1, q2 = self.access2q[g]
            Q1, Q2 = self.temp_mapping_dict[q1], self.temp_mapping_dict[q2]
            layer_factor = self.extended_layer_index.get(g, 0) + 1

            deps = self.dag_dependencies_count[g]
            e_distance += (deps+1) * \
                self.distance_matrix[Q1][Q2] * 1/layer_factor

        H = max_decay * (f_distance / front_layer_size + W *
                        ((e_distance / extended_layer_size) if extended_layer_size else 0))
