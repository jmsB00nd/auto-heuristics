**Algorithm structure:**

1. **Interaction graph construction** — Builds weighted edges between logical qubit pairs from 2-qubit gates, with exponential layer decay (earlier gates matter more).

2. **Top-K anchor pairs** (K=8) — Selects the highest-weight logical qubit pairs as seed candidates.

3. **BFS greedy expansion** — For each anchor pair × top-20 hardware edges (both orientations), maps the anchor pair then greedily expands by:
   - Picking the unplaced logical qubit with highest total interaction to already-placed qubits
   - Mapping it to the best adjacent physical qubit (minimizing weighted distance)
   - Expanding candidate physical qubits from radius-1 to radius-2 as needed

4. **Best seed selection** — Keeps the expansion with lowest total weighted distance cost.

5. **Fill phase** — Remaining unmapped logical qubits are placed greedily by interaction weight, using explicit exclusion sets to guarantee bijectivity.

6. **Local search refinement** — Full pairwise swap local search followed by simulated annealing, then a final local search pass.

**Guarantees:**
- Strict 1-to-1 mapping via explicit `placed`/`pq_used` tracking
- Polynomial time (K × edges × n for BFS, plus SA iterations)
- Populates both `self.mapping_dict` and `self.reverse_mapping_dict`
