Here's a summary of what the implementation does:

**Algorithm: Temporal-Decay Interaction Weighted Spectral Placement**

**Step 1 — Temporally-decayed QIG weights**
Each 2-qubit gate at temporal position `k` (sorted by gate ID ≈ circuit order) contributes weight `exp(-α·k/N)` to its edge in the Quantum Interaction Graph. With `α=3`, gates at the start of the circuit receive ~20× more weight than gates at the end.

**Step 2 — Weighted Laplacian & Fiedler vector**
Build `L = D - W` from the decayed weight matrix and compute its eigendecomposition via `numpy.linalg.eigh` (O(n³), efficient for typical circuit sizes). The Fiedler vector (2nd eigenvector) embeds logical qubits on a 1-D line that minimises temporally-weighted edge tension — pairs that interact early and heavily land close together on this line.

**Step 3 — Centrality-ordered physical qubits**
Physical qubits are ranked by mean BFS distance to all others (ascending = most central first). This mirrors the Fiedler embedding: the spectral "center" of the circuit maps to the hardware center.

**Step 4 — Align the two orderings**
The `rank`-th logical qubit in Fiedler order maps to the `rank`-th physical qubit in centrality order.

**Step 5 — Local search refinement**
Pairwise-swap hill climbing using the same temporally-decayed tension cost, converging to a local minimum consistent with the spectral objective.

**Step 6 — Strict 1-to-1 bijection**
The `mapping_dict` and `reverse_mapping_dict` lists are constructed with a displacement-safe swap to guarantee bijectivity across all `num_qubits` physical positions.
