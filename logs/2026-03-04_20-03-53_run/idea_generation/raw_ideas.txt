Continuing from GDELC's formulation:

```
H(SWAP) = max_decay × [
    Σ_{g∈F}  d_post(Q1,Q2) / |F|
  + W × Σ_{k=1}^{K}  r^k × ( Σ_{g ∈ layer_k_gates} d_post(Q1,Q2) / |layer_k_gates| )
]
```
Converges: geometric series Σ r^k ≤ r/(1-r) bounds the extended term. Distances decrease.
WHY_NOVEL: The baseline's 1/k decay has no principled motivation and creates a non-uniform gradient (layer 1→2 loses 50%, layer 9→10 loses 10%). Geometric decay maintains a **constant proportional discount** between adjacent layers, matching the RL discount-factor interpretation and enabling principled hyperparameter tuning.

IDEA_NAME: Residual Load-Proportional Priority Cost (RLPPC)
DESCRIPTION: For each gate g, weight its distance contribution by the geometric mean of its two qubits' total remaining gate loads — the number of unexecuted gates each qubit still participates in. A qubit appearing in 20 future gates needs routing priority now because its placement affects all future routing decisions; a qubit with 1 future gate is nearly retired. This creates a dynamic, load-aware prioritization that updates naturally as gates are executed.
MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION:
```
load(q, t) = |{ g not yet executed : q ∈ g }|   [updated each time a gate executes]

load_weight(q1,q2,t) = sqrt(load(q1,t) × load(q2,t)) / max_load(0)²   ∈ [0,1]   [normalized by initial max]

H(SWAP) = max_decay × [
    Σ_{g∈F}  d_post(Q1,Q2) × (1 + β × load_weight(q1,q2,t)) / |F|
  + W × Σ_{g∈E}  d_post(Q1,Q2) × (1 + β × load_weight(q1,q2,t)) / (layer_idx × |E|)
]
```
β ∈ (0,1]. Converges: load_weight ∈ [0,1] is bounded; loads decrease monotonically as gates execute.
WHY_NOVEL: The baseline uses `deps+1` (static in-degree: how many gates feed INTO g). RLPPC uses the dynamic REMAINING TOTAL LOAD per qubit (how many gates the qubit still needs to participate in going FORWARD). These are structurally different: a qubit with high in-degree but almost all predecessors executed has low remaining load; RLPPC captures this while the baseline cannot.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 6
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 6
EFFORT_SCORE: 8
AVERAGE_SCORE: 6.7
STATUS: KEPT
ELIMINATION_REASON: N/A

IDEA_NAME: Topological Bottleneck Quotient Cost (TBQC)
DESCRIPTION: Divide each gate's hop-count distance by the number of edge-disjoint paths between Q1 and Q2. Same distance, fewer parallel paths = higher cost.
MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION: H = Σ_g d(Q1,Q2) / (1 + EDP(Q1,Q2)) / |F|
WHY_NOVEL: Would capture routing flexibility beyond hop count.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 5
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 4
EFFORT_SCORE: 6
AVERAGE_SCORE: 5.0
STATUS: ELIMINATED
ELIMINATION_REASON: By Menger's theorem, the number of edge-disjoint paths between two nodes equals the minimum edge cut, which equals the max-flow with unit capacities. This is therefore mathematically equivalent to the Maximum Flow Routing Pressure Cost (MFRPC) listed in IDEAS NOT TO TEST.

IDEA_NAME: Successor Fan-Out Urgency Cost (SFUC)
DESCRIPTION: Weight each gate's distance by its immediate DAG out-degree (number of direct successors). Gates that directly unblock many other gates get higher priority.
MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION: H = Σ_g d(Q1,Q2) × (1 + fanout(g)) / (Σ_g (1+fanout(g)))
WHY_NOVEL: Uses immediate successors instead of layer index.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 5
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 6
EFFORT_SCORE: 8
AVERAGE_SCORE: 6.3
STATUS: ELIMINATED
ELIMINATION_REASON: Strictly weaker version of CCWWC. Immediate fan-out (depth=1) is a coarse approximation of causal cone width (all transitive dependents). CCWWC subsumes this idea and provides a richer signal.

IDEA_NAME: Harmonic Mean Layer Distance Cost (HMLDC)
DESCRIPTION: Aggregate front-layer distances using harmonic mean instead of arithmetic mean, emphasizing gates that are close to executable.
MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION: H = max_decay × [|F| / Σ_{g∈F} (1/d_post(Q1,Q2)) + W × e_dist/|E|]
WHY_NOVEL: Harmonic mean penalizes large distances more gently, rewards bringing any gate to d=1.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 6
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 4
EFFORT_SCORE: 8
AVERAGE_SCORE: 6.0
STATUS: ELIMINATED
ELIMINATION_REASON: The harmonic mean is dominated by the smallest distance values. This creates a perverse incentive: once one gate reaches d=1, the harmonic cost drops sharply regardless of the remaining gates. The cost landscape becomes flat for large-distance gates, removing the gradient signal needed to route them efficiently.

IDEA_NAME: Qubit Contention-Penalized Routing Cost (QCPRC)

MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION: contention(Q) = |{g ∈ F∪E : Q ∈ shortest_path(Q1,Q2)}|; H = Σ_g d × (1 + α × max_contention_on_path) / |F|
WHY_NOVEL: Vertex-level congestion vs edge-level.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 5
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 5
EFFORT_SCORE: 6
AVERAGE_SCORE: 5.3
STATUS: ELIMINATED
ELIMINATION_REASON: Too closely related to Edge Congestion Anticipation Cost (ECAC) which appears in IDEAS NOT TO TEST. In sparse coupling graphs (degree ≤ 3, as in heavy-hex), vertex contention and edge contention are nearly equivalent. The non-convergence risk noted for ECAC applies here too.

IDEA_NAME: Graph Distance Distribution Entropy Cost (GDDEC)
DESCRIPTION: Minimize the Shannon entropy of the distribution of front-layer distances. Low entropy with distances concentrated at 1 indicates ideal state.
MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION: freq(d) = fraction of F-gates at distance d; H = -Σ_d freq(d) × log(freq(d))
WHY_NOVEL: Targets homogeneity of progress rather than total progress.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 7
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 3
EFFORT_SCORE: 7
AVERAGE_SCORE: 5.7
STATUS: ELIMINATED
ELIMINATION_REASON: Does not converge. Entropy is not monotonically decreasing during routing: moving one gate from d=3 to d=2 can either increase or decrease entropy depending on the other distances. A SWAP that brings heterogeneous distances toward uniformity (e.g., [1,2,5] → [2,2,3]) may decrease entropy while worsening the routing. No guaranteed minimum aligned with "all gates executable."

IDEA_NAME: One-Step Predictive Extended Layer Cost (OSPELC)
DESCRIPTION: Replace d(Q1,Q2) in the extended layer with max(0, d(Q1,Q2) - 1) — the "anticipated distance after one more optimal SWAP."
MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION: H = max_decay × [Σ_F d_post/|F| + W × Σ_E max(0, d_post-1)/(layer_idx × |E|)]
WHY_NOVEL: Two-step lookahead for extended layer.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 5
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 5
EFFORT_SCORE: 9
AVERAGE_SCORE: 6.3
STATUS: ELIMINATED
ELIMINATION_REASON: Mathematically equivalent to the baseline for SWAP selection. Since max(0, d-1) = d - 1 for all d ≥ 1 (extended-layer gates always have d ≥ 1), the extended layer cost becomes Σ_E (d-1)/... = Σ_E d/... - constant. The constant does not vary with SWAP choice, so argmin H is identical to the baseline. No new information is introduced.

MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION: slack(g) = ALAP(g) - ASAP(g); deadline_pressure(g) = exp(-slack(g)/τ); H = Σ_g d×(1+deadline_pressure(g))/|F|
WHY_NOVEL: Uses circuit scheduling slack rather than raw critical path.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 5
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 5
EFFORT_SCORE: 7
AVERAGE_SCORE: 5.7
STATUS: ELIMINATED
ELIMINATION_REASON: Critically similar to Critical Path Gate Urgency Cost (CPGUC) in IDEAS NOT TO TEST. Both use circuit-depth-based timing analysis (ALAP/ASAP scheduling and critical path are closely related). Gates with zero slack ARE on the critical path. The distinction is too narrow to represent a fundamentally different approach.

IDEA_NAME: Non-local Qubit Interaction Tensor Cost (NQITC)
DESCRIPTION: Build a 3-tensor T_{ijk} = 1 if qubits i,j,k all interact within a circuit window. Use Tucker decomposition to find routing-relevant qubit clusters.
MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION: H = Σ_{(q1,q2)∈F} d(Q1,Q2) × cluster_mismatch(q1,q2,π)
WHY_NOVEL: Captures 3-body qubit interactions beyond pairwise structure.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 9
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 3
EFFORT_SCORE: 2
AVERAGE_SCORE: 4.7
STATUS: ELIMINATED
ELIMINATION_REASON: Computationally infeasible for real-time routing. Tucker decomposition is O(n³) per update; the tensor itself has O(n³) entries. For circuits with 50+ qubits, both memory and runtime are prohibitive. No evidence that 3-body interactions improve routing over pairwise signals sufficient to justify this cost.

IDEA_NAME: Gate Serialization Risk Cost (GSRC)
DESCRIPTION: For each candidate SWAP, detect if it forces two currently-parallel gates to become serialized (by blocking a shared physical qubit). Penalize SWAPs proportional to the number of parallel gate pairs they serialize.
MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION: serialize_risk(SWAP) = |{(g1,g2) ∈ F×F : g1∥g2 currently, but NOT after SWAP}|; H = distance_term + γ × serialize_risk(SWAP)
WHY_NOVEL: Directly targets circuit depth increase from serialization.
NOVELTY_SCORE: 7
PROBABILITY_SCORE: 5
EFFORT_SCORE: 6
AVERAGE_SCORE: 6.0
STATUS: ELIMINATED
ELIMINATION_REASON: High oscillation risk. A SWAP that temporarily serializes two gates in round t may be necessary to enable 3 gates to execute in round t+1. The serialize_risk term creates a myopic penalty that fights against globally optimal solutions. Without multi-step simulation, this term is likely to cause cycling and poor convergence in practice.
